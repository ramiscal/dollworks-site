THE DOLL WORKS '83

Privacy policy · Effective 4 October 2026

The short version: the game collects nothing. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The game never sends anything about you anywhere. What it saves stays on your iPhone.

What the game doesn't do

  • It doesn't ask you to sign in or create an account.
  • It doesn't show ads or include any advertising, analytics or crash-reporting code.
  • It doesn't track you across apps or websites, and it doesn't use your advertising identifier.
  • It doesn't connect to any server of ours. The only time it goes online is when Apple's App Store handles a purchase.

The microphone

In one part of the game, the Kiln Cellar, a doll hunts by sound. The first time you go down there, iOS asks whether the game may use the microphone. If you allow it, the game measures how loud your room is, a single number, so the doll can react when you make a noise.

The sound itself is never recorded, stored or sent. The game reads only the loudness, the audio goes nowhere, and listening stops as soon as you leave the cellar. If you say no, you can still play: the doll hears only your footsteps and the pebbles you throw. You can change your answer any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone.

Other things the game reads from your phone

These are used live, while you play, and none of them is saved or sent:

  • Motion (how you tilt the phone) to aim your flashlight and to lower a doll into its grave.
  • The proximity sensor (when you cover the top of the phone) to hide.
  • Your screen brightness setting and whether headphones are connected, to suggest turning the brightness down and wearing headphones.
  • Your phone's clock, because the mill is only open from 8 PM to 5 AM, your local time.

What's saved on your iPhone

When you rewind the tape at the end of a shift, the game saves your progress on your phone: the nights you've worked, the children you've laid to rest, and the initials you signed in the caretakers' log. It also remembers a few settings (such as the view and sound) and whether you've bought the full game.

This stays on your device. It's included in your phone's own backups, like any app's data, and it's deleted when you delete the game.

Purchases

The full game is an in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, Apple Account, payment details or address. The game asks Apple only whether this device's Apple Account owns the full game. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If a future version of the game ever changes any of this, we'll update this page and its effective date before that version is released.
