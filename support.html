THE DOLL WORKS '83

Support

Something wrong, or a question? Open an issue on this site's GitHub page. Say which iPhone you have and which floor you were on.

It says CLOSED FOR THE DAY

That's on purpose. The Doll Works is open from 8 PM to 5 AM, by your iPhone's own clock. By day the title screen counts down to opening time. If the clock passes 5 AM while you're on a shift, the shift ends and you clock out.

Getting the most out of it

  • Headphones. Footsteps and other sounds come from the left or the right, so you can tell where a doll is without seeing it.
  • Brightness down, lights off. The title screen tells you if your screen is too bright.
  • Hold the phone the way you'll play when you clock in. That's the angle the flashlight treats as straight ahead.

The flashlight doesn't aim where I tilt

The beam's resting angle is set when you clock in, so start the shift holding the phone comfortably. You can also put a finger on the scene to point the beam.

Hiding

Stand in an open grave and cover the top of the phone, where the proximity sensor is, near the earpiece. The screen goes dark while you hide. If that's awkward, hold the HIDE button instead.

The microphone

In the Kiln Cellar the game asks to use the microphone so the doll there can hear how loud your room is. It measures loudness only and records nothing (see the privacy policy). If you said no, you can still play; the doll hears your footsteps and the pebbles you throw. To change your answer: Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone › DOLL WORKS.

Saving

The night is saved when you rewind the tape after you clock out. If you close the game mid-shift, that shift isn't saved, but the children you laid to rest on earlier saved nights stay at rest. Saves live on your iPhone and are deleted if you delete the game.

The Full Cartridge

The Moulding Room and the Painting Room are free. The Full Cartridge is one in-app purchase that opens the rest of the mill: the Kiln Cellar, the Hedge Maze, the Burying Ground, the Crypt and the Sewing Loft. There are no ads and nothing else to buy.

New phone, or reinstalled? The game checks with Apple each time it opens, so if you're signed in with the Apple Account that bought it, the whole cartridge unlocks by itself. If it doesn't, tap RESTORE on the cartridge screen, which appears when you try to go past the Painting Room. For refunds, use Apple's Report a Problem; Apple handles all payments.
